## 📖 Created Mnemonic

**Status:** ✅ Complete

Once upon a time, there was a tiny computer, so small it fit in your hand, called a Microcontroller. This Microcontroller had a brain, its CPU, which was like a master chef, and a Memory, which was like a recipe book filled with instructions.  To interact with the world, it had little legs, called Pins, that acted like tiny light switches.  The Microcontroller's programs were the recipes that told its brain what to do.  These recipes instructed the pins when to flip their switches ON (HIGH) or OFF (LOW).  These pins, acting as switches, were how the tiny computer controlled other things, like turning a light on or off, or making a motor spin, all by following its recipe!
