## 📖 Created Mnemonic

**Status:** ✅ Complete

Once upon a time, in a world of tiny gadgets, lived a little robot with a powerful brain. This brain, called a CPU, was the main thinker, constantly receiving instructions from a super-fast note-taker named Memory. Together, they formed a tiny computer, ready to do amazing jobs. To interact with the world, this tiny computer had special connection points, like tiny hands, called pins. These pins acted like miniature light switches, capable of being turned ON (HIGH) or OFF (LOW) with incredible speed.  A special boss, known as a program, held the secret messages and instructions that guided the CPU. The CPU would read these instructions from Memory and then command the pins to flip ON or OFF, controlling everything from making a light blink to making a motor spin. By rapidly switching these pins, the tiny computer could orchestrate a symphony of actions, bringing its robot to life and completing its tasks.
