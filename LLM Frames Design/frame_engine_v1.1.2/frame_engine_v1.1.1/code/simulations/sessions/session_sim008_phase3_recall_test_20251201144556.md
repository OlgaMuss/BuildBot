## 📖 Created Mnemonic

**Status:** ✅ Complete

Once upon a time, there was a tiny computer, a Microcontroller, that was like a bossy brain. This brain had tiny people called pins, who acted like little arms or mailboxes. These pins needed instructions, like secret codes, from the bossy brain to know what to do. However, the pins were like empty mailboxes, sad and unable to work until they received power, like batteries. When the secret codes arrived, the pins could then turn ON or OFF, like a light switch, or HIGH and LOW, to sort and send their 'mail', allowing the tiny computer to do its job.
