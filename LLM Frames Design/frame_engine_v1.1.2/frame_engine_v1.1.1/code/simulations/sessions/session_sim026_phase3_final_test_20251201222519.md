## 📖 Created Mnemonic

**Status:** ✅ Complete

Why did the microcontroller break up with the recipe?
Because it felt like it was always being told what to do, including when to do it!

The CPU is the chef, and the memory is the pantry, full of ingredients for the program's recipe.

Why did the chef (CPU) get confused in the pantry (memory)?
Because the recipe (program) wasn't specific enough about *when* to add the ingredients!

Why was the microcontroller so forgetful?
Because its brain (CPU) was too small to remember the whole recipe at once! It needed the recipe uploaded every time.
